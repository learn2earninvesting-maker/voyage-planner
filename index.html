Port rotation

    
Pick each port in sailing order (type to search about 12,900 ports, harbours and anchorages, or enter your own name and lat/lon, e.g. 48 18.0N / 069 24.0W or decimals). “Route via” forces the route through a point, such as Cabot Strait; “Leg break” starts a new leg without a stay, such as a pilot station. Distances come from the free searoute network for bunker estimates only, not for navigation: replace them with passage-plan figures once the plan is drawn.

    

    

    
Port data: NGA World Port Index (Pub. 150), GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and a UN/LOCODE sea-ports list. Points you enter yourself are kept under ★ My ports on this device.
